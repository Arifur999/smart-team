# Market research

Facts the campaign will repeat in public must be true. Every fact here gets a source URL, or it does not exist.

## Sources, in order

1. The client: brief, website, existing reviews and posts.
2. Customers' own words: reviews (Google, Trustpilot, app stores), Reddit and forum threads, comments.
3. Competitors: their sites, ads (Meta Ad Library, Google Ads Transparency), offers and prices.
4. Platform and industry data from reputable publishers (with dates).

Use deep-research when it is loaded for broad questions.

## Write `01-research/research.md`

- **Customer**: who buys, the pains in their own quoted words (with links), what triggers a purchase.
- **Competitors**: a table of name, main message, offer, price if public, channel, what they do well, the gap.
- **Platforms**: what formats and hooks work now on the briefed platforms (dated sources).
- **Gaps to own**: 3 angles competitors leave open.
- **Usable facts**: a numbered list of facts the copy may use, each with source and date. Mark anything older
  than 2 years.
