# Campaign strategy

A strategy is a set of decisions, not a list of ideas. Every line answers "so the team will do what?".

Write `02-strategy/STRATEGY.md` with:

1. **Objective and KPI**: one objective from the brief (sales, leads, followers, awareness) and the number that
   proves it, with a realistic target and how it is measured.
2. **Audience**: who, what they want, what stops them, the words they use (from `01-research/research.md`).
3. **Positioning**: one sentence: for <audience>, <brand> is the <category> that <benefit>, because <proof>.
4. **Key messages**: exactly three, each with proof from the research (a sourced fact or a client asset).
   No proof, no message.
5. **Offer and CTA**: what the customer gets and the single next step.
6. **Channel plan**: a table of channel, role (reach, trust, convert), format, frequency, dates. Only the
   platforms in the brief.
7. **Budget split** when a budget was given: per channel, with the reason.
8. **Tone**: three adjectives with a do/don't example line each, inside the brand voice.

Also write `02-strategy/rubric.json`: the marketing-qa dimensions (strategy, message, voice, accuracy, channel,
search, cta, polish) and the must-pass items for this campaign (every fact sourced, every briefed channel
covered, brand words to avoid). End with at most 3 questions for the client's Ready check.
