# Social content

Each platform has its own grammar. A post that fits one feed looks lazy on another.

## Calendar (`04-content/vN/calendar.md`)

A table: date, platform, format (single image, carousel, reel, story), hook (first line), caption, CTA,
hashtags (3-8, mixed reach), visual brief, which key message it serves. Spread the messages; one CTA per post.

Platform notes: Instagram feed 1080x1350, story/reel 1080x1920, carousel up to 10 slides with the hook on slide
1; Facebook feed 1080x1350 or 1080x1080; LinkedIn 1200x1500 or 1080x1080, professional first line; TikTok
1080x1920, hook in the first second.

## Visuals

Make the 3-6 most important post visuals as PNG at the right size in `04-content/vN/`: build them in HTML with the
brand tokens, real fonts and the real logo, then screenshot with a headless browser. Text on visuals is short,
large and inside the safe area (no text in the outer 8%). Never use stock people or invented testimonials.

Use prompt-master only if the client asked for AI-generated images, and say which images are generated.
